Daylight under cover

Daylight under cover

The right cover. The right amount of light.

A solid roof keeps the rain off but leaves the space underneath dim. Translucent sheets do both jobs: they give you a practical overhead covering and let daylight through, so porches, terraces and workshops stay bright and usable.

  • Natural daylight brightens covered areas without extra lighting during the day.
  • Lightweight sheets suit a wide range of roofing structures.
  • Choose the tint: clear for maximum light, coloured for a softer glow.

FAQ

Common questions

Which sheet is right for a car porch?

Most porches use translucent or corrugated fiberglass sheets. Clear lets the most light through; blue, green and amber give a tinted, softer light. Send us a photo of the porch on WhatsApp and we'll suggest options.

What colours are available?

We commonly supply clear, blue, green and amber tints, plus decorative finishes. The exact range changes with stock, so ask us for what's available right now.

How do I get a price?

Use the quote form above or message us on WhatsApp with the product, colour and the area you want to cover. We'll reply with options and a quotation.

Can I see the sheets before buying?

Yes. Visit our shop at 5A Ferozpur Road, near Mozang, Lahore to see sheets, colours and profiles in person.

Do you supply commercial and workshop projects?

Yes. Translucent roofing works well for workshops, shop canopies and other commercial covers. Share your requirements and quantities and we'll quote accordingly.
